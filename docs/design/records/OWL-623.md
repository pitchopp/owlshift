# OWL-623 · Show how many tickets wait for a parked holder under `policy.overlap = "exclusive"`

Settles: [architecture](../architecture.md) section 5, "A stopped holder yields", *Shown*, its second sentence; split off from [OWL-621](OWL-621.md).

## As built (2026-11-22)

- *Who waits.* The tickets whose latest told wait (`why::Folded::current`) is a collision naming the parked ticket as its holder, one of this runner's tickets: a wait behind a person's ticket, a pull request or another runner's claim is not counted (`why::waiting_for`). A waiter counts while the drain still reads it, in flight or listed by `ready.json` (`why::DrainReads`, the rule `why` and `status --waits` already applied, now one copy), and while it is neither parked nor waiting for an answer. Words: `why::kept_words`, grouped by resource, five tickets named per resource and ten resources, the rest counted.
- *Which holder.* A parked ticket that keeps what it holds: every parked ticket under `exclusive`, and under `rebase` one that did not yield (a whole-repository record). `why::keeps_nothing` is the one test of a yield that holds nothing, shared by `why`'s standings, the digest, `status --waits` and the park.
- *The surfaces.* The PARKED comment (`ParkedComment::waiting`, `**N tickets wait for it**`), filled by `OnDemand::kept_waiting` at the park from the event log; `owlshift why`, the parked line of the project's view and a line of `why TICKET`; the digest, `HOLDER keeps N tickets waiting: …` within its parked part, five holders named; the web UI's card through the parked row's `wait.text` of `owlshift status --waits`, which the command prints too. No contract or format changes.
- *Tests.* Bench, scenario S5: `under_exclusive_a_parked_holder_keeps_a_second_build_waiting`, DEMO-2's admission wait told while DEMO-1's Build runs, then the four surfaces read after DEMO-1 parks. Runner: `the_tickets_waiting_for_a_holder_are_counted_and_grouped_by_resource`, `a_parked_wait_is_worded_by_voice` (a yielded holder tells none), the digest's line test.

## Built otherwise than designed

- *The PARKED comment says who waits when the ticket parks*: the tracker adapters edit no comment, so a ticket told to wait after the park is shown by `why`, the digest and the UI, which read the events as they stand.
- *The count is a lower bound and may lag*: a collision wait names the first holder its check found, so a ticket two holders keep back counts for one; and a told wait stands until its ticket is dispatched or runs, so a holder's narrowing (OWL-461) or delivery shows in the count at the waiter's next pass, as the waits of `why` and `status --waits` always did.
- *A log or refs that do not read* give the PARKED comment no line rather than failing the park; the digest says what it cannot read as before.
- *A project whose events are spelled in two cases* is counted under the latest spelling (`why::spelled`, moved from `status --waits`).
