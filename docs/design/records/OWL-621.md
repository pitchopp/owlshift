# OWL-621 · Let a parked or long-waiting ticket yield what it holds under `policy.overlap = "rebase"`

Settles: [architecture](../architecture.md) section 5, "A stopped holder yields" and the "Who goes on" line that names it; [build plan](../build-plan.md), "P6 · Parallel", part 15, but for its `exclusive` display.

## As built (2026-11-21)

- *The record.* `held.json` is format 4 (`TicketHeld::yielded_at`, `rebase_due`, and `HeldOverlap::resources`); formats 1 to 3 read as not yielded and refuse the new fields. A record yields only what it holds and never the whole repository (`TicketHeld::yieldable`, refused by `validate`).
- *Who yields.* `owlshift_core::collision::yields` decides, `ROUND_YIELDS_AFTER_MS` the six hours, from the latest ask's `at`. The park writes the yield in `OnDemand::park_with`, the one park point, and watch's pass writes it for a round in its sixth hour (`Watch::yield_stopped`), deciding again from the record read under the project's and the ticket's lock.
- *What a yield is.* The core's `InFlight::yielded`: `collision::weigh` leaves a yielded Owlshift ticket out of conflicts, the bound of two and the overlaps under `rebase` alone, and names what a ticket takes of it as a `Shared` marked `yielded`: the inner paths (the whole repository taking the yielded ticket's own) and the named resources both hold. The admission (`weigh_admission`, shared by the Build's admission and the admission again) and the widening record it with the overlaps (`held::record`), told as `yield` = `taken`. A taking the record cannot keep (100 overlaps) holds nothing back: it is warned of (`overlap_unrecorded`).
- *The resume.* `OnDemand::admit_yielded` admits a yielded ticket again in one section before its next run, its whole set weighed `Dispatched::Now` and as not delivered: called by `continue` before a stage (after its slot check), by `do` after its dispatch, and at Watch before a repair or a correction runs. A conflict stops with `Stop::Waits` at the stage, the record still yielded. Watch's pass predicts it for stage steps (`Watch::collisions`, a `schedule::Due` in the ticket's dispatch order). Admitted, the record loses the yield and, its pull request not open, keeps `rebase_due`; its claim is pushed again.
- *The rebase.* `rebase_due` is decided at the next Build step, in `before_base` (`OnDemand::after_yield`): when the base changed what the ticket's overlaps reach since its branch parted from it (`held::Reach`, named resources by their patterns), a branch with commits of its own is rebased by one `rebase` role run as a held Build's is (`rebase_held` with `Off::Yielded`, the brief's `rebase.yielded`, event `yield` = `rebase`), one with none is fast-forwarded, and either is dispatched again; a kept done Build's result follows the rebased head, as after a held Build's rebase. A failed rebase parks (`ParkReason::Rebase`) with `rebase_due` kept. Watch's refresh reads the same reach, its cache keyed on it, so a taking recorded on unchanged heads is read.
- *Shown.* The PARKED comment's `**It yields what it held**` line (`Yielding`, ten names then a count), `owlshift why` (each ticket's yield, takings, admission again and rebase; the project's view telling a stopped ticket that yielded from one that keeps what it holds; the overlaps' named resources) and the digest (`N parked (…, K of them yielding what they hold)`).
- *A switch to `exclusive`* (found in review on 2026-11-21): under `exclusive` a yielded record holds its whole set again, in `weigh`, watch's prediction, `admit_stage`, the claim and `why` and the digest (`project::overlap_at_origin_head`); its yield's time stays for a switch back, and its next run clears it without a check of its own (`admit_yielded`), its stage's check by dispatch order being made already. A ticket that took one of its paths under `rebase` holds it beside it, and the one dispatched first goes on. Test: `under_exclusive_a_yielded_holder_holds_its_whole_set_again`.
- *Tests.* Core: `a_yielded_holder_holds_nothing_and_its_taking_is_told`, `under_exclusive_a_yielded_holder_holds_its_whole_set_again`, `a_parked_ticket_or_a_round_six_hours_old_yields`. Contracts: `a_ticket_held_yields_what_it_holds_and_keeps_a_takings_resource`, the rebase brief's kinds in `brief_rejections`. Runner: `held`'s record, reach, claim and refresh tests, `a_parked_comment_says_what_its_ticket_yields`, `a_yield_and_each_taking_are_told_for_the_ticket_that_yielded`, the digest's line. Bench, scenario S5 (`yield_scenarios.rs`): a parked Build yielding its migration chain, waiting while the taker holds it, then rebased onto the taker's merge and delivered; a round yielding from its sixth hour, its answer finding its file shared; a parked holder under `exclusive` keeping a second Build waiting.

## Built otherwise than designed

- *A ticket resumed at Design or Verify* is not rebased before that run, but at its next Build step: `rebase_due` waits for it there. A Verify pass that passes delivers the branch unrebased, and Watch's refresh then rebases it once the base changes a path its overlaps reach. The rebase is not made before a Verify pass, since it would replace the Build that pass reviews.
- *"Since it yielded"* is read as since the branch parted from the base: the base's changes since that merge-base, on what any of its overlaps reaches, a tolerated overlap recorded before the yield included, trigger the rebase. No base commit is kept at the yield.
- *The rebase brief* names the new kind by a field (`rebase.yielded`) within brief format 30: a format bump would rewrite every role file's front matter, which a ticket built in parallel was editing. `roles/rebase.md` says what the field means.
- *The overlap* is recorded once, by the taker, and read from both sides (`held::sharings_of`), as a tolerated overlap is (OWL-449). A taker whose ref is gone, or whose pull request closed unmerged, no longer counts, so the resumed ticket is then not rebased for it.
- *Watch's pass also yields a parked record not yet yielded*, under `rebase`: a park made before this binary, or while the switch was `exclusive`.
- *A whole-repository record never yields*: a taking of it would name no path, and no rebase or refresh would follow.
- *Named-resource overlaps are not in the Build's brief*: `BriefOverlap` lists paths, and a field would bump the brief's format.
- *A delivered ticket's repair*, while its admission again waits, is refused at its step (`Stop::Waits`) and tried again after watch's retry delay; watch's pass predicts only stage steps.
- *Remote claims (P10)*: a yielded record's claim publishes nothing held from its next push or renewal, under `rebase`. Another runner's taking records no overlap here, so a resumed yielded ticket's rebase reads its own held set too, its named resources by their patterns (`held::resume_reach`, found in review on 2026-11-21): at most a rebase more, its gate on the combined code whoever took what it held. Test: `under_remote_claims_a_resumed_ticket_is_rebased_on_its_own_set`.
- *A resumed Build with uncommitted paths* is not rebased: a `warning` (`yield_not_rebased`), added to the design's accepted residuals.

## Split off

Under `exclusive`, the number of tickets waiting for a parked holder, and for what, in its PARKED comment, `owlshift why`, the digest and the UI's card ("Shown", second sentence) is not built here: split off to a ticket of its own on 2026-11-21, the ticket allowing it, since it changes no state and touches four other surfaces.
