# OWL-619 · Let a plan hold a person's steps: build the rest, deliver, and ask the decider

Settles: [architecture](../architecture.md) section 4, "A person's steps"; [build plan](../build-plan.md), "P5 · Quality pipeline", part Y.

The ticket is built in three pull requests, each on a branch of its own: part 1 the plan, the Build, the delivery's listing and a hold on merge on green; part 2 the round at Watch for the `before_merge` steps, a defect it reports starting a fix run, and the follow-up filed at the merge for the `after_merge` ones; part 3 the handback of a ticket with no step for a run.

## As built, part 1 (2026-11-22)

- **The plan.** `PlanStep` gains `by` (`agent` when absent, never written for an agent's step) and `when` (`before_merge` or `after_merge`), a person's step's alone (`crates/owlshift-contracts/src/review.rs`). A plan of agent steps alone therefore renders, and hashes, as before: a kept approval's `plan_sha256`, a published plan's parts and a review pass's print are unchanged by the upgrade. `Plan::check` refuses a person's step without `when`, an agent's step with one, and a step due before the merge after one due after it. Formats: `plan.json` 2 (format 1 read, refused when it says a person's step or `owed`), `result.json` 16, the brief 31; the nine role prompts name them.
- **What the Build owes.** The design says the Build's ledger records a passed step as owed; the ledger is the run's own file, which the runner does not read, so a `done` result names in `owed` the agent's steps of its given plan that wait on a person's step's result. The runner checks them against the brief's plan (each a step of it, the agent's, after a person's step, in increasing order; only from the build role, with a plan, with `done`) and keeps them in `plan.json` (`TicketPlan::owed`), whose step numbers they index, so a new plan starts with none. The Build runs of one plan add to it and never remove: a step a later run builds after all stays listed, which costs a line, where a run that forgets one would hide unbuilt work. `TicketPlan::owed_steps` gives every person's step and every owed agent's step, the latter due when the nearest person's step before it is: a rule the design leaves open ("owed with it").
- **`person_step`.** `owlshift_core::gate::PERSON_STEP`, kept for the decider by `GatePolicy::always_human` at every delegation level, so `route` sends it to the decider with no resolver run, and a resolver decision labelled so is refused (`route_decided`). It is not a `FloorCategory`: no level hands it over, and merge on green's question hold lifts once its decider answers, as for any category kept for a person. `roles/build.md` asks a person's step met outside the plan under it, never `blocked` for it; that behaviour rests on the prompt, the runner checking only the routing.
- **Delivery.** The pull request's body is the agent's text, then "For a person": each owed step with when it is due, a person's with the result to report (`writer::pull_request_body`), both through the closing-keyword neutralising, the tracker's closing line last. The first delivery's DELIVERY report lists the same steps in what remains for a human; a repair's report does not repeat them. The plan's document marks each person's step and lists them apart, and the PLAN comment adds them to its counts line.
- **The interim hold.** Until part 2's round, nothing asks the decider for the results: merge on green waits on `MergeBlocker::PersonSteps` while a step due before the merge is owed, a hold kept for a person (`merge_waits`, blocker `person_steps`, told once per head and notified), and the DELIVERY report and the delivery line say that Owlshift will not merge the pull request and that a person reviews and merges it. A person's step answered as a `person_step` question lifts only its own question's hold. After the delivery the plan does not change: the core has no way back from Watch to Design.
- **An all-person plan.** Until part 3, `Plan::check` refuses a plan with no step of the agent's, and `roles/design.md` tells the design run to end `blocked` on such a ticket, saying the work is a person's whole: it parks at Design, as such a ticket parked before at Build, now with no Build run spent.
- **Prompts.** `roles/design.md` says when a step is a person's (no run can do it with a Build's permissions; a check in a browser until browser QA is built) and how to split the work; `roles/design_review.md` questions the split as any step; `roles/build.md` passes a person's step, marks it owed in its ledger and names the agent's steps left in `owed`; `roles/verify.md`, which the design does not name, gains one sentence so that an owed step's absence from the diff is no finding.
- **Tests.** The plan's rules, the format-1 read and the rendering of an agent's step (`a_ticket_plan_round_trips_and_refuses_what_it_must`, `an_agent_s_step_renders_as_before_and_format_1_is_read`); the result's `owed` (`a_build_owes_only_the_agent_s_steps_after_a_person_s_step`); the gate's table at every level (`each_level_routes_its_categories`, `a_decision_needs_a_basis_the_level_allows`); `merge_due`'s hold beside an answered `person_step` question (`each_condition_alone_blocks_the_merge`); the writer's section, report lines and plan (`the_steps_owed_a_person_are_listed_where_a_person_reads`); on the bench, `a_plan_whose_last_step_is_a_person_s_is_built_delivered_and_held_from_merging`, `a_person_s_step_met_outside_the_plan_is_asked_of_the_decider` and `a_review_loop_build_that_changes_nothing_keeps_what_it_owes` (a review loop's Build that commits nothing keeps its `owed` too).

## As built, part 2 (2026-11-22)

- **The round.** At the first read at Watch that asks for no repair (green, checks running, or a red the base's), after the correction and the review loop, watch runs a step of its own (`StepKind::PersonSteps`, no model, so no usage limit holds it) and `continue` at Watch does the same (`on_demand/person.rs`): the runner's own round, through no resolver, one question a person's step due before the merge, category `person_step`, its option A the result the plan names (the recommendation, so a thumbs-up answers it) and B what it gave instead. Not inside the delivering command: one path at Watch, whose failed post the next pass retries. `plan.json` format 3 keeps each step asked (`checks`: step, round, question) in the write that keeps the round, and the RESUME's write keeps how each was answered: option A `passed`, any other answer, option B or the decider's own words, `defect`. While the round waits the ticket waits at Watch, as for any round: a red gets no fix run until the decider answers.
- **The run.** Decided by the maintainer on 2026-11-22 and written in [architecture](../architecture.md) section 4, "A person's steps": the round's answers are the instruction of one run of the build role at Watch (brief format 32, `person_steps`: each answered step, its question, whether it is a defect and the answer check's reading; the decider's words are in the thread), which fixes each defect and builds the agent's steps of `owed` whose person's steps before them all passed. A defect never releases an agent's step: its step is asked again first, then a later run builds it. The run's push is a correction's (`PushKind::Correction`, landed with the core's `Corrected`: no fix pass, no repair, failed runs bounding it), its kept report naming `steps` instead of questions (`delivery.json` format 9) and its DELIVERY report leading "Changed after a person's steps". Once it lands, in the same write, or when adopted after a crash, `TicketPlan::settle_steps_run` drops the defects' checks, so those steps are asked again in a new round, and the built steps leave `owed`. A step answered as passed is never asked again. A run given defects alone may end `done` with no commit, reported "No change after a person's steps" and its steps asked again; one given steps to build that commits nothing is a failed run. Not a new push kind, which would have bumped `proposals.json` for a run the correction's machinery already carries.
- **Merge on green.** The round and the run come before the merge's facts in the same pass, so `MergeBlocker::PersonSteps` (now: the person's steps due before the merge not answered as passed, and the agent's steps still owed before it) is reached only when neither a round nor a run is due, a state only a bug or a hand-edited ref makes: it stays a hold kept for a person, notified, as in part 1. Its interim words left the delivery line and the DELIVERY report, which say again that Owlshift merges once green unless a person must judge first; the pull request's "For a person" section says the round, the run and the follow-up.
- **The follow-up.** Not at delivery: once the delivery ended merged, by the runner's merge or a person's, the filing that runs after the end (`watch/ended.rs`, and `continue`'s settling) owes and files one proposal for the plan's steps due after the merge (`proposals::after_merge`): titled after the ticket, so never another's duplicate, each step in its evidence, for its decider in its text. The tracker adapters assign no proposal, so it names the decider without assigning them. It is not counted among the three a parent files (`Filing::exempt`), so a ticket whose runs filed three still files its person's steps. A closed pull request, or one someone else pushed to, files none. A person who merges while the round waits, or with agent's steps still owed, settled the round (OWL-242): only the steps due after the merge are filed.
- **Part 1's residual.** An agent's step the run after a round builds leaves `owed`. One a later review loop's Build builds still stays listed, since a Build's `owed` only adds.
- **Limits.** A passed result was given on the head it was asked at: a later run for another step's defect does not ask it again. A defect cycle (answer, run, asked again) is bounded by the decider's answers, not by a count.
- **Tests.** The plan's checks, their rules and the format-2 read, and the life of a person's steps down to the invariant that whatever is owed has a round or a run due (`a_person_s_steps_are_asked_answered_and_settled_by_their_run`); the brief's `person_steps` (`brief_quality_pipeline_fields_go_to_their_roles_only`); the report's steps and the format-8 read (`a_ticket_delivery_round_trips_and_is_checked`); the lead lines (`the_run_after_a_person_s_steps_names_them_in_its_lead`); the follow-up (`a_merged_ticket_owes_one_follow_up_for_its_steps_after_the_merge`); on the bench, `a_plan_whose_last_step_is_a_person_s_is_built_delivered_and_held_from_merging` (asked at watch's first read, nothing merged while the round waits, step 3 built by the run, then merged, no hold told), `a_defect_reported_in_the_round_starts_a_run_and_its_step_is_asked_again` and `the_steps_after_the_merge_are_filed_as_one_follow_up_once_merged`.
