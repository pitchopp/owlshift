# OWL-620 · Send a blocked run through the resolver before a person is asked

Settles: [architecture](../architecture.md) section 4, "A `blocked` through the resolver"; [build plan](../build-plan.md), "P5 · Quality pipeline", part Z. Follows [OWL-619](OWL-619.md), whose owed person's steps the `person_step` verdict uses.

## As built (2026-11-22)

- **The rule.** `owlshift_core::unblock::open` gives the verdicts open at a stage: `retry`, `rebase` where the stage can rebase, `person_step` where it can owe a step, and `park` always. Once `retry` or `rebase` was taken at a stage of the ticket, `park` alone is open there, so a second `blocked` after either parks, with the resolver's sentence. `person_step` closes nothing. `unblock::delegated` sends a `blocked` to the resolver from `discoverable` up.
- **The run.** `OnDemand::unblock` (`on_demand/unblock.rs`). No resolver run below `discoverable`, after the decider took a false premise's closure (`resolver::closure_taken`, read from the latest ask's verdict), at the ticket's budget or at the chain's bound: the ticket parks.
  - Otherwise one resolver run, on the run `OnDemand::run_resolver` now shares with the question rounds' `resolve`. Its brief gives `asked_by` (role, run, summary, the plan as its steps stand, the branch's diff) and `unblock` (stage, open verdicts, the run's ledger). It has no `resolve`.
  - Its result gives `unblock`: verdict, note, category, basis, kind, quote, and a `person_step`'s step and `when` (`UnblockAnswer`). The contract refuses a verdict that is not open, and a step that is not an agent's step of the given plan.
  - The run counts in the chain's cost. An acting verdict counts one settled pass.
- **The decision.** The verdict becomes a decision on the runner's own question, category `unblock`, one option per open verdict (`resolver::unblock_question`, `unblock_resolution`). `resolver::settle` holds it to the level as any decision: the resolver's label, the basis kind, a quote.
  - `log_decisions` posts it as a DECISION comment and keeps it, with the act (`KeptDecision.unblock`, questions.json format 20), in one write.
  - A `person_step` also hands the step in the plan in that same write (`TicketPlan.handed`, plan.json format 4). The plan's text and every hash stay as written: `TicketPlan::effective` reads the step as a person's for what is owed, asked and built.
  - A `park` posts no DECISION. Its note leads the PARKED comment (`ParkedComment.first`, "**First, for a person:**").
- **The acts.**
  - Build at Build, review loop included: `retry` runs the stage again. `rebase` goes through `rebase_held` (`Off::Unblocked`) by way of `onto_base`, which `after_yield` now shares; the Build is dispatched again on the rebased branch. The rebase brief says why with `rebase.unblocked` (brief format 33), as it says `broken` or `yielded`, and `roles/rebase.md` has "An unblocked Build". `person_step` runs the Build again, given the step as a person's.
  - `rebase` is open only outside a review loop, on a branch not delivered and clean, that does not hold the base's head as this command fetched it. `person_step` is open only with a given plan holding an agent's step.
  - Watch (`repair_runs`: fix, correction, person's results, rebase runs): `retry` and `park`. A `retry` counts the attempt, so a trivial rebase's second run leaves the fast tier.
- **Shown.** A `decision` event `unblock` with the verdict, stage, role and resolver run. A park the resolver did not give carries `reason` (`pass_limit`, `budget`, `cost_unreported`, `ticket_budget`, `failed`, `usage_limit`, `refused`, `unposted`). A `person_step` carries `step` and `when`. `roles/resolver.md`, "A run that stopped `blocked`", describes the verdicts.
- **Tests.**
  - The core's rule table.
  - The contracts: brief, result, questions.json and plan.json rules and older formats.
  - The resolver's verdict held to the level, and the closure read.
  - The PARKED comment's first line.
  - On the bench, `unblock_scenarios.rs`:
    - a misread permission retried and delivered;
    - a second `blocked` after a `retry` parked with the resolver's sentence first;
    - a `blocked` at `facts` parked as before;
    - a chain at its bound parking without a run;
    - a Build behind its base rebased and delivered on the combined code;
    - a last step owed to a person and delivered;
    - a fix run at Watch retried.

## Built otherwise than designed

- **`rebase` is closed at Watch.** Section 4 gives `rebase` to a Build-role run without naming the stage. At Watch the branch is delivered, and its rebase is `repair_rebase`'s, reached from watch's own read of the pull request. Giving the verdict there needs a rebase cause of its own in that path, so it is left to a follow-up.
- **The held Build's pre-Build rebase run (`rebase_held`, OWL-427) keeps today's park.** Its `blocked` ends through the rebase's own failure (`RebaseFailed`), not through the blocked path the other runs share, so it gets no resolver run. The rebase run that the `rebase` verdict itself starts parks the same way.
- **A reversed verdict is not undone.** The decider's reply to its DECISION comment reverses it through the existing reversal path: the next run follows the reply, or a correction run after delivery. A `person_step` reversed after delivery still lists its step as owed.
- **A handed step may be one the Build already committed.** The runner checks only that the step is an agent's step of the plan; the resolver reads the ledger to pick the step the run stopped at.
