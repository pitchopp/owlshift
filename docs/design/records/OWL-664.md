# OWL-664 · Let a project merge a green pull request once its decider answered every security question

Settles: [architecture](../architecture.md) section 5, "Merge on green", "No question left for a person", "A project's choice for `security`"; section 8, the floor's paragraph; section 12, D14.

## Decisions (2026-11-27)

The ticket left two questions to its decider, answered on 2026-11-27:

- *Q1, the `irreversible` floor before an answer:* unchanged. An `irreversible` question holds until its decider answers it, under either setting; only what happens to `security` after an answer changes.
- *Q2, `variant:risky`:* forces `person` whatever the project setting, the variant read as dispatch reads it, and the words say the ticket is `risky`.

## As built (2026-11-27)

- *The setting.* `policy.security_merge` (`Policy::security_merge`, `owlshift_core::vocab::SecurityMerge`): `person` when absent, or `answered`; any other word is refused naming the key. It is read at the base commit beside `[forge] merge` and `merge_method`: `project::forge_merge_at` returns one `MergeSettings` (watch's `follow::Switch`, cached per base commit as the switch was). A branch that sets it changes nothing for its own ticket (`a_branch_cannot_turn_merge_on_green_on`). A separate reader, as `overlap_at` is, was weighed: it parses the project file a second time for what the switch read already holds.
- *The rule.* `owlshift_core::follow::SecurityRule` (`Person`, `Risky`, `Answered`), `SecurityRule::of(setting, variant)`: `person` gives `Person` whatever the variant, so a default project's `risky` tickets keep today's words; `answered` gives `Risky` on a `risky` ticket, else `Answered`. `MergePolicy::security` carries it, and `MergeBlocker::Security(category, rule)` names it. `held_question(gate, security, questions)` skips, under `Answered` alone, a `security` question, the category's or the resolver's label (`security_category`), settled by its decider (`Settled::Decider`); it then falls to the human categories' pass, where an answered question holds nothing. `Settled::Resolver`, a resolver's decision or a recommendation the runner took, reversed or not, and `Settled::Open` still hold. `security_lifted` lists the questions so lifted.
- *The variant.* `merge::merge_variant`: the ticket's `variant:` label, else intake's verdict, else `pipeline.default`, as `routing::ticket_variant` reads them, except that any `variant:risky` label counts even beside a disagreeing `variant:` label, which dispatch reads as none (found in review: a stray second label would have lifted the `risky` exception). `merge::security_rule` reads it under `answered` alone. Watch's merge pass takes it from the labels it read and the ref read under the lock; `continue`'s merge note and the delivery line from what the command read; a DELIVERY report posted by watch's settle path reads the labels from the tracker, under `answered` alone, a failed read reading none (the report's words only: the merge pass reads them itself).
- *What is said.* `merge::hold_words` names the rule: `person` adds "(the project's `policy.security_merge` is `person`)"; `risky` says "a person merges a security question of a `risky` ticket whatever the answer, though the project's `policy.security_merge` is `answered`"; `answered` says the question has no answer of its decider on record, or was decided without its decider, and that a person merges it. Those words reach the DELIVERY report, the delivery line, `continue`'s note, watch's line, the notification and the `merge_waits` event's `reason`, which `owlshift why` shows; the event also gets `security_merge`. With nothing holding, `merge::lifted_words` follows who merges in the DELIVERY report and the delivery line (`MergeSaid::lifted`, `writer::merges_on_green`'s `lifted`): "Under the project's `policy.security_merge = "answered"`, its decider's answer lifted the hold of question Q1 of round 1 (asked under `security`)." No date: a verdict keeps no time, and one would need a `questions.json` format bump. The runner's own merge records `security_merge` and `security_lifted` (each question's id, round and category) on its `pr_merged` event (`merge::security_data`), under `answered` alone. `owlshift config show` prints `security_merge` and its file, or `default`, in the always-human section, saying that merge on green reads it at the base branch's head.
- *Tests.* Core: `answered_lets_the_decider_s_answer_lift_a_security_hold`, the existing tables under `Person`. Contracts: `the_security_merge_is_a_policy_key_person_by_default`. Runner: `the_hold_names_its_question`, `a_lifted_security_hold_is_said_and_a_risky_label_always_counts`, `the_resolver_settings_are_shown_with_their_source`, `a_branch_cannot_turn_merge_on_green_on`, the DELIVERY report's merge lines. Bench (`merge_scenarios.rs`): `answered_lifts_an_answered_security_question_but_not_a_risky_ticket_s`, one bench: the lifted sentence at delivery, a `variant:risky` label put on holding the merge with one `merge_waits` (`security_merge` `risky`) and its notification, the label taken off and the merge with `security_lifted`.

## Built otherwise than designed

- *A merge found by a later read* (`watch.rs`'s read of a pull request merged while the ref kept the runner's merge under way, `on_demand.rs`'s same read at a command) records no `security_lifted`: the rule in force at the merge is not kept in the ref, and keeping it would take a format bump of `delivery.json`'s `merging`. The runner's own merge, the one watch decided, records it.
- *The CHANGELOG* is written at release, as 0.3.0's was; no line was added here.
