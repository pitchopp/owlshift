# OWL-626 · Show a handed-back ticket as handed back in status, why and the UI

Settles: [architecture](../architecture.md) section 5, "`owlshift why`" and "`owlshift status`" (the OWL-626 revisions); the limit "Status, `why` and the web UI show a handed-back ticket at Ready, with no word of its own" of [OWL-619](OWL-619.md), part 3.

## As built (2026-11-22)

- **What was read.** `handback.json` keeps its time alone, and the `handback` decision event is local to one machine, so `handed_back::of` tells what the run read from what the ref keeps beside the handback, with no contract change: a plan with no agent step whose `text_sha256` is the intake record's (or with no intake record) is the design run's, its steps `TicketPlan::owed_steps`; otherwise the intake record's `person_only` quote; otherwise such a plan; otherwise nothing, worded "its HANDBACK comment says what was read". A ref may keep both a quote and a person's plan: a ticket handed back at intake and admitted again on the same text goes on to Design with that record, and its plan may be handed back in turn, hashed as the record is; a plan hashed otherwise is older than the record, the text having changed after a design handback, which ran intake again. A ticket is shown handed back only while its state is at Ready, as `continue`'s refusal reads it.
- **The gesture.** The HANDBACK comment's words (`readmit_words`) of the project file the project's `served.json` keeps, the one its watch runs on, read once per read and only when a ticket is handed back; "make the project's admission gesture again" when none reads. The terminal's words add `owlshift do TICKET`; the web UI's, which has no button for it, do not.
- **The surfaces.** `status --waits` keeps a handed-back ref as a row though it neither holds nor is listed (`RefDetail::handed_back`), its wait `handed_back` from the ref, over a told one, `since` the handback's time, shown `[ready, handed back]`; it holds no capacity and does not count as waiting for a parked holder. The web UI's ticket page, overview board and graph read that row (the graph's state `other`, as a park's). `owlshift why TICKET` says it on its `now:` line, then each person's step as the comment lists them (`writer::handback_step_lines`, shared with the HANDBACK comment); `owlshift why` gives it a line of its own instead of its told wait. `owlshift status` has a part of its own, `handed_back`, off the Orchestrator page as `parked` is.
- **Limits.** Between a person's re-admission gesture and the drain's next pass, which drops the handback at its dispatch, every surface still names the gesture to make. A plan of a person's steps alone and an intake quote kept on the same text, the plan from a Design whose text changed under it after the intake, read as the design's: that needs an intake handback, a re-admission and an edit during Design.
- **Tests.** `what_was_read_is_told_from_the_ref` (the rule, the Ready state, both voices); `a_handed_back_ticket_is_shown_as_handed_back` (status --waits: the row out of flight, over a told wait, not counted for a parked holder, its line); `a_handed_back_ticket_is_told_over_its_last_wait` (`why`, both views); `a_followed_pull_request_at_watch_is_in_review` and `the_claims_are_listed_with_their_operator_and_lease` (`owlshift status`: the sort and the line); `a_handed_back_tickets_wait_names_the_gesture` (the web UI's waits card, the served file's gesture); on the bench, `a_plan_of_a_person_s_steps_alone_is_handed_back_with_no_build` and `a_ticket_intake_reads_as_a_person_s_is_handed_back_and_admitted_again_after` read status, `why`, `why DEMO-1`, `status --waits` and the UI's card after the handback, and nothing handed back once the ticket is admitted again.
