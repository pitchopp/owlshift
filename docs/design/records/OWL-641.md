# OWL-641 · Give .NET restores one NuGet lock across concurrent runs

Settles: [runtime and operations](../runtime-and-operations.md), "More stacks (OWL-624, OWL-627, OWL-628, OWL-629)": the rule for a run cache gains a clause for a variable that only orders concurrent runs, and the .NET row gains `NUGET_SCRATCH`. It supersedes, for that kind of variable alone, the "on macOS at least" of [OWL-624](OWL-624.md)'s "The rule for a variable", and [OWL-632](OWL-632.md)'s decision "`init`'s .NET lines are left as they are" (its "Two runs at once"). Neither record is edited.

## As built (2026-11-23)

- `stack::dotnet` writes `run_caches.NUGET_SCRATCH = "nuget-scratch"` after `DOTNET_CLI_HOME`, with a `# note:` of its own saying why and that it was checked on Linux, as pnpm's `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR` has (OWL-629). The notes that give the lines without writing them (several solutions or projects, `global.json` alone) name both variables.
- **The rule.** A variable that only orders runs sharing a run cache, such as a lock's folder, cannot fail a single run: one run alone was green without `NUGET_SCRATCH` in 16 of 16 runs (OWL-632's 6, the 10 below). It is now written when two gates at once on emptied run caches are red in most pairs without it, green in every pair with it, at least ten pairs each way, a single gate green with it set. Its evidence may come from one system, the other named pending here.
- **Held by** `stack::tests::a_dotnet_root_restores_and_tests_its_one_solution_or_project`, `init::tests::a_maven_composer_and_dotnet_root_is_written_and_loads` (the variable loads back from the written file and passes the run-cache names check), `every_run_cache_detected_passes_the_names_check`.
- A project file written before this change keeps the race until its line is added by hand: `init` does not rewrite an existing file. [`recipes.md`](../../recipes.md) says so.

## Live check R1, Linux (2026-11-23)

Ubuntu 24.04.5 x86_64 in a cloud container, bubblewrap 0.9.0, uid 1001, .NET SDK 10.0.404, `owlshift` built from `main` at this branch's base. Rig: [`owl-627.sh`](../live-checks/owl-627.sh) `dotnet` with `CONCURRENT=1`, unchanged, started 20 times from 11:42Z to 11:58Z, alternating the `[stack]` as `init` wrote it and the same with `STACK_EDIT='s/^run_caches.DOTNET_CLI_HOME = "dotnet"$/&\nrun_caches.NUGET_SCRATCH = "nuget-scratch"/'`. Each start makes the fixture, runs the gates alone, then one pair on an emptied run cache tree (each gate its own `OWLSHIFT_DATA_DIR`, one `OWLSHIFT_CACHE_DIR`), then a third gate alone.

| `[stack]` | Pairs with a red gate | Red gates | Third gate alone | One gate, `NUGET_SCRATCH` alone removed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| as `init` wrote it on `main` | 9 of 10 | 13 of 20, at `dotnet restore`: `Could not find file '<run cache>/dotnet/.nuget/packages/<package>/<version>/<random name>'` or `Could not find a part of the path`, the package varying over ten (`microsoft.testplatform.testhost` three times, `coverlet.collector` twice, `xunit.core`, `newtonsoft.json` among the others) | green 10 of 10 | |
| with `NUGET_SCRATCH` | 0 of 10 | 0 of 20 | green 10 of 10 | green 10 of 10 |

The one pair green as written was the first; the rig empties the tree before each pair (`rm -rf`), so it is read as a race lost by neither side, not a filled cache. NuGet wrote 8 kB into `nuget-scratch`. Then this branch's `init`, unedited, three starts (12:02Z to 12:05Z): it wrote `run_caches.NUGET_SCRATCH = "nuget-scratch"` and its note; 3 of 3 pairs green, the third gate and the single gate without the variable green each time.

The measures stay per system: OWL-632's on Ubuntu aarch64 were 5 of 10 pairs red as written and 6 of 6 green with the variable; these, on x86_64, 9 of 10 and 10 of 10.

It decides: `init` writes `NUGET_SCRATCH` for .NET, on Linux's evidence under the rule's new clause.
